## Competitor 1: Bennett University — Clubs & Societies

**URL:** `https://www.bennett.edu.in/bennett-life/life-at-campus/club-and-societies/`

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| Primary Purpose | Official university directory of student clubs and societies |
| Primary Audience | Prospective students, current students discovering clubs |
| Domain Authority | High (official .edu.in domain) |

### Strengths
- Official university backing = authoritative for SERP rankings
- Lists 25+ clubs across university and sports categories [OBSERVED]
- Accessible under the trusted `bennett.edu.in` domain

### Weaknesses / Content Gaps
| Gap | Detail |
|-----|--------|
| ❌ No search or filtering | Cannot filter clubs by category or interest |
| ❌ No upcoming events per club | Clubs listed in isolation from their events |
| ❌ No contact or social links | Students must search Instagram/WhatsApp separately |
| ❌ No join/register CTA | No clear call-to-action for membership |
| ❌ Minimal descriptions | 1–3 sentences per club [INFERRED from search snippet depth] |
| ❌ No achievement highlights | No competition wins, member count, or activity level |
| ❌ Static page | Not updated dynamically; events not reflected |
| ❌ 4-level deep URL | `/bennett-life/life-at-campus/club-and-societies/` — crawlability risk |

## Differentiation Strategy

### 1. Own the "BU Student" Search Intent
Unstop is a national platform; bennett.edu.in is the official source. **Neither provides the holistic, student-centric, BU-specific discovery experience** that Bennett Student Hub targets.

### 2. Event Schema → Rich Snippets
Implementing `Event` schema on individual event pages and `FAQPage` schema on the FAQ page can unlock Google's event carousels and PAA boxes — giving Bennett Student Hub SERP visibility even before domain authority builds.

### 3. "How to Register" as a Utility Page
The "Bennett University event registration" query is currently answered poorly across multiple disconnected sources. A comprehensive, always-current `/faq/` page directly answering this question can capture this intent definitively.

### 4. Club Contact Details — The Biggest Gap
Zero competitors provide club head contacts, WhatsApp links, or verified social handles. This information is scattered across Instagram and WhatsApp. **Bennett Student Hub aggregating this creates unique, non-duplicable value.**

### 5. Hyper-Local SEO
Targeting "Greater Noida" and "NCR" location modifiers consistently across all pages fills a gap that national platforms ignore and the official university site doesn't optimise for.
