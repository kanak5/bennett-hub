Hackathon Registration at Bennett University: A Student Guide

Published 26 November 2026 · Bennett Student Hub · Student Resource Guide

Hackathons give university students an opportunity to turn ideas into working projects, collaborate with others and develop practical technical skills. For Bennett University students, knowing where to find upcoming hackathons and how to complete hackathon registration can make it easier to participate in campus and inter-college competitions.

Whether you are participating for the first time or already have development experience, this guide explains how to discover events, register successfully, form a team and prepare for the competition.

Explore Bennett Student Hub: Discover campus events, explore technical clubs, and find student workshops to build skills before your next hackathon.

What is a hackathon?

A hackathon is a time-limited collaborative event where participants work individually or in teams to develop a solution to a particular problem. Projects can include websites, mobile applications, artificial intelligence tools, automation systems and other technology-based solutions.

Participants generally work within a fixed time period and present their completed projects to judges or organisers. Learn more about the hackathon concept and its history.

Where to find hackathons at Bennett University

Students should regularly check multiple channels because hackathon announcements may be shared by different clubs, departments and external organisers.

  • Technical clubs: Follow coding, artificial intelligence, cybersecurity and development club announcements.
  • University announcements: Check official university notices and department communications for upcoming student activities.
  • Campus event listings: Browse the Bennett Student Hub events section for event-related information.
  • External platforms: Explore platforms such as Devfolio and Unstop for inter-college and national-level competitions.

How hackathon registration usually works

Registration procedures vary between organisers, but most events follow a similar process.

  1. Read the eligibility requirements. Check the event theme, team size, participation rules and registration deadline.
  2. Visit the registration platform. Open the official registration form or event listing shared by the organisers.
  3. Create or sign in to an account. Use an email address that you regularly check.
  4. Enter participant details. Provide the requested information, which may include names, university details and contact information.
  5. Complete team registration. Follow the organiser's instructions about individual and team-leader registration.
  6. Save the confirmation. Keep the confirmation email and registration details for future communication.
Registration tip: Do not wait until the final day. Some hackathons limit the number of teams, and completing registration does not always guarantee selection.

How to form a successful hackathon team

A balanced team can combine different skills to develop a more complete project. Depending on the competition, useful roles include:

  • Frontend developer: Builds the user interface.
  • Backend developer: Handles server-side logic and APIs.
  • Designer: Plans the interface and user experience.
  • Presenter: Organises the demonstration and explains the idea.

Team members should discuss their strengths, assign responsibilities and agree on a realistic project before the event begins.

What to prepare before a hackathon

  • A laptop, charger and reliable internet connection.
  • Development tools and software installed in advance.
  • A shared GitHub repository for project collaboration.
  • Basic knowledge of the technologies required for the project.
  • A clear understanding of the event rules and judging criteria.
  • A short presentation and demonstration plan.

Common hackathon registration mistakes

  • Missing registration deadlines.
  • Ignoring team-size and eligibility restrictions.
  • Entering incorrect participant details.
  • Failing to check confirmation emails.
  • Not completing registration for every team member when required.
  • Waiting too long to decide on a project idea.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to be a computer science student?

Not necessarily. Some hackathons welcome participants from different academic backgrounds, including design and business. Always check the specific eligibility requirements.

Is hackathon registration free?

Many campus events are free, but registration fees may apply to certain external competitions. Check the official event listing.

Can I participate without a team?

Some organisers allow individual participation or provide team matching opportunities. Confirm this before registering.

What should I do after registering?

Save your confirmation, join the official communication channel, review the rules and coordinate with your team.
